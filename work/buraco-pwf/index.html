Buraco: Play with Friends

Rebuilding our social matchmaking experience 🃏

Overview

Buraco is a traditional card game in Brazil and Gazeus' biggest product. It started over 10 years ago as a Flash game for desktop browsers, then launched on iOS and Android in the following years. It has over 400k daily active users, more than 10 million downloads and a very loyal user base.

Role

Senior UI/UX Designer

Gazeus Games

Rio de Janeiro - Brazil (Remote)

2020

The Opportunity

The pandemic hit and our whole year's roadmap went down, with the company moving to remote work and adapting its process.

Our users changed as well, playing more online matches and chatting more. We saw an opportunity in this and decided to remake an old feature called Play with Friends. It had a lot of potential, and with a new UX we could give our users an easy way to invite their friends and play.

Discovery Process

Immersion

Brainstorming

We used Mural to choose and prioritize which features were essential to this new version. We also drafted our users' journey in these sessions.

Benchmark

In this group exercise, each member of our team brought a good (or a bad) multiplayer lobby creation experience. This let us look at positive and negative experiences from different perspectives, and identify a few pain points to avoid.

Flowchart & Wireframe

Two different user flows:

  • Host: the player who creates the lobby, invites their friends and organizes the pairs (in a 4 player match).
  • Guest: the player who receives an invitation through another messaging app or social media.

User Testing

Before releasing this feature to our users, we organized an internal user testing session. The feedback helped us identify some pain points and bugs. We took the prototype back to development, made all the changes and improvements we had found, then ran a new round of user testing to validate them.
