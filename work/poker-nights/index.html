Design Process

Market Research

I used Game Refinery to find popular Poker games in the US segment, with an age filter between 15 and 35 years old.

I also filtered by the most profitable to the lowest, and decided to cross the results to see if there was any big difference between the games that were popular with a younger public, to the top revenue games.

Sohoo Poker was a game that really stood out in this user base, but I think that the aesthetics and design decisions are more focused on the Chinese market, and wouldn't be that popular with a broader audience.

Market Research @ Game Refinery

Benchmark

I used Miro to create a few boards and organize my findings, from screenshots to visual elements and references that would help me develop a concept. During benchmark, I noticed that most Poker games had a dark interface, with light effects and a lot of skeuomorphism. I really liked this Casino and neon lights theme, and decided to create an UI with that aesthetics.
