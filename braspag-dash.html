Braspag Dash

A Design System for Braspag's dashboards

Overview

Our team decided to create a Design System for the dashboards, defining our identity across all products. I adopted the Atomic Design System methodology to create the hierarchy and its elements.

In addition to the visual and usability improvements, all the components developed were reused in other products, creating a standard and maintaining consistency across the entire ecosystem.

This way, a design and frontend team could provide the entire interface, while the product teams could focus on backend improvements and bug fixes.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Braspag / Cielo

Rio de Janeiro - Brazil (On-site)

2017

The Problem

The lack of identity for Braspag's products was a major challenge. Each project had a different interface, created by third-party companies, that not only didn't follow our brand's visual standard, but during a specialist analysis we detected major UX issues in almost every heuristics evaluated.

Components Library

Color Palette

Brand colors (Braspag Blue, success and alert), the Split project color, and status colors for sales (authorized, captured, cancelled) and transactions (scheduled, error, pending, liquidated, in and out).

Buttons

Primary and secondary actions in normal, hover, pressed and disabled states, plus positive and negative actions.

Text Inputs

Default, active, disabled, error, success and warning states, plus inputs with calendar, percentage and search icons.

Dropdowns

Default, active, disabled, error, success and warning states, matching the text inputs.

Radio Buttons

Default, hover, active and disabled states.

Checkboxes

Default, hover, active and disabled states.

Toggles

Yes/no toggles for positive and negative answers, and on/off switches.

Alerts

Primary, secondary, dark, success, error and warning alerts, plus an alert with an icon.

Tooltips

Error, light informative and dark informative tooltips.

Text Styles

Nunito Sans type scale: three headline levels and body text, with their sizes, weights and emphasis.

Header Icons

Featured illustrations for page headers: wallets, price tags, branding, components, design, message, success and expired link.

Icons

Line pictograms for navigation and status, such as financial calendar, settings, dashboard, notifications, reports, users and sales, based on Font Awesome Pro 5.1.0.

Page Loader

Loading state with a progress bar and placeholder blocks while the page loads, based on Pace.js.

Tables

Table header and table rows.

Progress Bar

Progress bars in the primary color, positive and negative variants, and a version that shows the percentage.
